**Welcome to Miri Regev's World !** <br>
A fun and addictive obstacle game developed for Android.

In this exciting obstacle race game, you control Miri's car as it speeds down the highway. <br>
Your mission is to collect as many of Miri’s Colleagues as possible. <br>
As you navigate the road, you'll face obstacles in the form of Miri's personal <br>
driver who will try to slow you down and decrease your lives. <br>
Once you lose, your score is saved, and if you perform well, you'll enter the leaderboard.

At the beginning of the game, you can choose your preferred driving speed and mode - buttons or sensors.

Test your reflexes and try to beat your best score and secure a spot in the Top-10! <br>
